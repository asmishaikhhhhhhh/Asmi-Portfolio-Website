### p20 — My Learnings (hand-drawn sketch slide)
1. "Clarity is Power" — "Design is not about adding— it's about removing." Too much… → Just enough. "In complex systems, users struggle because there's too much." "I distill complexity into clear, intuitive, and usable experiences." "Clarity isn't just good design— it's a competitive advantage."
2. "System > Individual apps" — "The most valuable thing I learned is that great design doesn't live in isolated screens — it lives in systems." "Because if the system breaks, no individual screen can save it." "And that's where real impact lies."
3. "Empathy" — "What is stressing them right now?" "What can I remove to make their job easier?" "How can this design give them confidence, not confusion?" "Good design doesn't just serve users — it supports them."
