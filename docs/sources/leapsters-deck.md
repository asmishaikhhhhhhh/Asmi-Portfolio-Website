## 3. The three geometric shape characters (for animation)

**Names / personalities: NOT STATED anywhere.** They are not named, and they are separate from the app mascot "Koko" (see the end of this section). How they are used: a decorative brand / emotional device on opening, emotional, problem and closing slides. They never appear in the board-game or app UI slides.

**Colours** (sampled from the renders; ±a few units; no hex values are printed for them):
- Pink (circle, always): **~#EABAC2**
- Yellow-green: **~#E9EBB5**
- Light blue: **~#C3D9FB**
- Line / feature colour (eyes, glyphs, limb lines): dark slate **~#314957**
- No outlines: the bodies are flat fills.
- **The colour assignment changes between slides.** On p18 and p20: **triangle = yellow-green, circle = pink, square = blue**. On p1, p2 and p41: **square = yellow-green, circle = pink, triangle = blue**.

**Construction (best reference: p18 standing row, p20 totem; 4000-px crops checked):**
- **Triangle:** isosceles, fairly tall, all three corners well rounded (apex rounded). Face sits in the upper third: two solid round dot eyes spaced apart, with one continuous stroke between them (round caps, about the width of the eye dots). The stroke goes **down vertically, turns right for a short horizontal, then runs diagonally down-left**, like a small "5" or "ら". This combined nose/mouth mark is the only feature: no separate mouth, no eyebrows, no cheeks or blush. **Arms:** two small rounded-rectangle nubs, the same colour as the body, sticking out diagonally from the middle of each slanted side and angled downward. Each has a thin dark line running along it parallel to the triangle's edge (it reads as the arm's inner contour). **Legs:** two short rounded-end rectangular stubs under the base, centred, with a small gap. No feet.
- **Circle:** a large circle. Face in the upper-middle: two dot eyes, and between them a stroke that goes **down vertically, then swoops into a rounded hook curving right and back to the left at the bottom**, like "ち". This is the closest thing to a smile. **Arms:** short vertical rounded nubs on the left and right sides at mid height, each with a vertical dark line on its inner edge. **Legs:** two short stubby rounded legs at the bottom.
- **Square:** a rounded-corner square (about as wide as tall, slightly taller in the totem). Face in the upper-middle: two dot eyes, with an **angular stepped glyph between them: down, right, down, left**, like a squared-off "5" or a bracket. **Arms:** vertical rounded nubs at mid height on each side, each with a vertical dark line. **Legs:** two stubby rounded legs, set wider and thicker than the circle's.
- **Expression overall:** calm, gently content, slightly quizzical. They are **not explicitly "smiling"**: there is no open mouth, no teeth and no blush. The single stroke glyph does the work of nose and mouth. All three share the same face language and differ only in the stroke's shape (diagonal / curved / angular), which echoes each body shape.
