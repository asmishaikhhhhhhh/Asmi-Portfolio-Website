## 2. Structured extraction

- **One-liner**: "Nurturing Confidence Through Play." Subtitle: "Bridging Foundational Learning for Future Generations" (01). Manual tagline: "Take small leaps with confidence." (27). Idea line: "Learning that adapts, supports, and connects without pressure." (22)
- **Context/background**: India; "Over 70% of Grade 3 students cannot read a simple Grade 2-level English text, this gap impacts their confidence." (02; source NOT STATED). Secondary research: academic papers, media reports (post-pandemic learning loss, digital divide, teacher gaps), NEP 2020, NCERT, UNICEF, UNESCO; competitor review of BYJU'S, Khan Academy, Diksha (05). External factors: uniform teaching methods, language mismatch, outdated pedagogy, overcrowded classrooms; internal: low confidence, learning anxiety, performance pressure, reduced classroom interaction (06).
