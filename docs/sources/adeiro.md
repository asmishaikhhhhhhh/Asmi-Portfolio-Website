## 2. Structured extraction (only what is visible)

- **One-liner:** NOT STATED as a single line in the images. Tagline: "Make Them Adore". Closest framing (02): "a fictional Portugal origin online store named "Adeiro" that manufactures and sells skin care products".
- **Brand brief (02, 04):** Fictional online skincare store, Portugal origin, plans to expand to India. Per 04 it "first started in 2003" with a minimalistic website "convenient for females to order supplies". Products range from cleanser, serum, age miracle cream and moisturizer to accessories, perfume and handbags. The design aims for a "natural and intense vibe". Visuals, typography and colour are "sophisticate, botanical, enriched and elegant". Colours are "earthy and mature".
- **Brand quote / values (04):** "Quality, comfort and safety." Desired user feelings (02): "warmth, comfort and intense care". Logo essence (05): "intense care".
- **Name meaning (05):** "The name "Adeiro" is a Portuguese word which determines reality." (Transcribed as stated; the slide gives no source.)
- **Target audience:** females ordering skincare online (04: "convenient for females to order supplies"); India mentioned as an expansion market (02). No age or psychographic detail given.
- **Personality adjectives:** sophisticate[d], botanical, enriched, elegant, natural, intense, earthy, mature (02); close to nature (05).
- **Moodboard:** NOT STATED (no moodboard slide).
- **Logo:** custom-looking display wordmark "ADEIRO". The A has a looped, rounded left stroke and a small serif crossbar, and the E/I pair shares a long horizontal stroke. Tagline "Make Them Adore" in a geometric sans. Monogram combines "A" and "D" (05). Primary logo = wordmark + tagline; secondary logo = monogram A (06). Construction grid with X/Y units: total 8X wide, 5X high; the monogram is 4.5X high (06). Clear space must be kept in all uses (06). Logo explorations or sketches: NOT STATED / not shown.
- **Scalability (07):** lockup at 100/50/25 mm; monogram at 50/25/15 mm.
- **Usage (08, 09):** white and black backgrounds; photo backgrounds; solid grey, olive, sage and taupe backgrounds.
- **Don'ts (10):** do not distort proportions, rotate, use a pop colour (red shown) or reduce opacity.
- **Colour palette:**
  - Primary "green": CMYK 16/0/13/70, RGB 64/76/66, HEX #404C42 (11). Use: highlight features and elements.
  - Secondary "Serenade": CMYK 0/6/11/0, RGB as printed 225/240/228, HEX #FFF0E4 (12). Use: contrast in design and typography. (The printed RGB and hex disagree.)
  - Other tones appear in mockups (sage, taupe, beige, olive, rose-gold) but have no specs.
- **Typography:**
  - Primary typeface: "SOAGE" (13; the slide also says "LOGO", which suggests it is the logo face, but that is not stated explicitly).
  - Secondary typeface: "Gilroy" (14).
  - Typographic usage (15): Header "LUSTRIA"; Sub header "GARET"; Body copy "GARET BOOK".
- **Imagery / photography:** warm, soft-focus portraits of women with natural textures (palm frond, foliage) on 01, 04 and 09; a bright studio skincare portrait on 02; real product photos on 35 and 36. Whether any photo was shot by the designer: NOT STATED (Behance lists "Photography" as a field, but no slide credits the photos). The 01, 02, 04 and 09 images look like stock, but that is not stated.
- **Packaging (23 to 27, 35, 36):** cream jar "ANTI AGINIG CELL BY CELL", carton box, dropper bottle with insert card, face wash 500 ml, skin mist toner 250 ml, deep cleanser 650 ml, tall box 250 ml, scented candle, white bottle. Line-drawn flower illustrations on the sage/cream/pink packs. Physical prototypes (35, 36): Under-Eye Serum 5ml, Face Serum 10ml, Nourishing Gel 50g, Exfoliating Scrub 50g, Firming Mask Pack 50g, with botanical-sprig labels.
- **Copy on packs:** "Made from 100% natural ingredients"; ingredients "Water, Lemon Extract, Flower Essence, Tea Tree Oil"; "HOW TO USE: Wet your face with water and use your fingertips to apply the product. Rinse with water and pat dry with a soft towel." (This copy names "Borcelle", a template placeholder.)
- **Applications / collateral:** corporate stationery set (16), business card 3.5 x 2 in (17, 18), A4 letterhead 8.27 x 11.69 in (19), envelope (20), hang tags (21), cards (22), gift card with T&Cs (29, 30), printed brand manual book (31 to 34). The contents page also lists "Merchandise".
- **Brand manual structure (03):** About Us 05, Logo 07, Typeface 15, Colours 18, Collaterals 22, Applications 29.
- **Learnings / reflection:** NOT STATED.
- **Duration:** NOT STATED.
- **Year:** "2023" on the cover (01); gift card valid until May 9, 2023 (29). Behance publish date Aug 2025 (from the brief, not the images).
- **Context:** "COMMUNICATION DESIGN" (01); "NMIMS Design Agency" / "NMIMS DESIGN" / www.nmimsdesign.com / www.nmimsagency.com appear on mockups and contact (16, 17, 20, 28, 32, 33). This suggests an academic (NMIMS) course project, but the slides never say so.
- **Tools:** NOT STATED in the images (Behance metadata says Illustrator and Photoshop).
